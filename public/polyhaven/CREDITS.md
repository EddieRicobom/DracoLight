| --- | --- | ---: |
| Utility Box 01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/utility_box_01 | 4,404 triangles |
| Utility Box 02 | https://polyhaven.com/a/utility_box_02 | 6,268 triangles |

License: https://polyhaven.com/license

The glTF meshes and 1K source maps were downloaded through Poly Haven's official
asset API on 2026-11-15 and checked against its MD5 hashes. For this game, the
three JPG maps for each model were resized to 512 × 512 pixels at quality 84;
the glTF image references were updated. Mesh geometry is unchanged. The module
`src/polyprops.js` instances a limited number of cabinets on roof chunks and
hides each cabinet when its supporting chunk is destroyed.
